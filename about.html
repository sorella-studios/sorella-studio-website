ABOUT SORELLA

Built on
sisterhood.

Sorella means “sister” in Italian. The name came from the encouragement between two sisters—and from the belief that beauty feels best when it is rooted in confidence, kindness, and real connection.

OUR BEGINNING

A personal idea
with a bigger purpose.

Sorella Studio began in Temecula as a family-centered dream: create a meaningful, flexible business while making customized beauty feel welcoming and attainable.

The vision was never a rushed “tan and go.” It was a calm, polished appointment where every guest is seen, listened to, and cared for—and where a beautiful result never has to come with an intimidating experience.

THE SORELLA STANDARD

Luxury in the
little things.

Every appointment starts with a conversation about your skin tone, undertones, desired color, event timing, and past spray-tan experience. Your tan is then customized around you, followed by clear rinse and aftercare guidance.

Every guest should feel welcomed with kindness, treated with professionalism, and leave more confident than when they arrived.

BEAUTY · CONFIDENCE · CONNECTION

Pretty, personal,
and never one-size-fits-all.

Sorella’s look may be polished, but the heart of the studio is warm, approachable, and real. The goal is simple: soft, even, natural-looking color and an experience you look forward to coming back to.
